Privacy policy

Kodin automaatio is a private home automation app used only by its owner.

The app accesses the owner's Google data (Sheets, Drive, Gmail, Calendar and Apps Script) only to run the owner's own home automation. The data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own devices.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.

Contact: the owner of this GitHub account.
